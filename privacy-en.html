Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026

RESPONSIBLE PERSON AND CONTACT

The person responsible for SpaceSweep is Felipe Henrique Baccaglini Custodio. Support and privacy contact: fhbcpbv@gmail.com.

SpaceSweep helps you review photos and videos and choose what to delete from your library. It requires no account, has no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs, and does not automatically send your library or logs to the app developer.

PHOTOS, VIDEOS AND PERMISSION

The app requests read and write access to your Photos library. You can deny access, allow only selected items, or allow your entire library. SpaceSweep accesses only the items iOS makes available according to your choice. You can change or revoke this permission in iOS Settings.

On your device, the app uses thumbnails, preview images, videos for playback and the metadata needed for its interface: item identifier, type, date, duration, resolution, screenshot status and media resources. It does not use photo location, facial recognition, identification of people or content analysis for advertising or tracking.

MEASUREMENT AND CACHE

Before measuring sizes, you choose between local resources only and including iCloud in that analysis. The app reads resources in chunks and discards the received chunks, without assembling complete copies in the app's storage. Including iCloud allows Photos to download complete originals, including videos: this may use significant cellular data, battery and storage. Prefer Wi-Fi and a charger. Network permission applies only to the requested analysis; returning to the app or library changes do not automatically start new downloads. You can pause; resuming asks for a new choice. Without downloading, resources that are not locally available may have unknown sizes. Network failures can also prevent measurement.

Sizes, identifiers and selections remain in memory. The Clear memory cache button clears the app's reusable size and thumbnail caches, pauses analysis and keeps displayed results and your selection. Visible thumbnails may reload. Downloaded originals and internal Photos/iCloud caches are managed by iOS: SpaceSweep cannot empty them with this button. Estimates represent logical bytes and do not promise recoverable physical storage. A previously cached size does not guarantee that all resources are still local.

LANGUAGE PREFERENCE

By default, the app follows the primary system language when it is English or Portuguese; other languages use English. In the app's settings you can keep the automatic option or choose English or Brazilian Portuguese. Only that language preference is stored in the app's own UserDefaults so it remains selected after reopening the app. It is not sent to the developer and is removed when the app's data is deleted according to iOS behavior. iOS permission and deletion dialogs may follow the language in system Settings.

ICLOUD AND NETWORK USE

The preview first tries to open content without network access. If content requires iCloud, the app shows a warning and asks you to choose whether to load it. This choice may use cellular data and store resources managed by Photos on your device. iCloud communication, synchronization and storage are provided by Apple's services and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. SpaceSweep does not ask for your Apple password or receive iCloud credentials.

DELETING FROM YOUR LIBRARY

No item is deleted automatically. You choose items, confirm a summary in SpaceSweep and respond to the iOS confirmation. In a synced library, deletion may also affect iCloud and other devices. Deleted items remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. SpaceSweep does not empty that folder; permanent deletion requires your action in Photos. Cancelling confirmation is not treated as an error.

LOCAL DIAGNOSTICS

The diagnostic panel stores up to 400 events from the last 7 days on your device, such as the app/iOS version, permission states, item/selection counts, operation results and error codes. Logs do not include photos, file names, item identifiers or complete error descriptions. Expired events are removed when logs are loaded or saved; there is no scheduled cleanup while the app is closed. Existing events keep the language used when they were recorded.

The log file uses iOS data protection and is excluded from backups. You can delete logs in the panel. Removing the app deletes its local storage according to iOS behavior; it does not remove your photos from the library.

SHARING AND SUPPORT

Sharing a log is optional and uses the iOS share sheet. Nothing is automatically sent to the developer. You choose the recipient and method. The recipient will have a copy, subject to the chosen service's practices. Review the text before sharing.

If you email support, the responsible person receives your email address and any information you choose to include in the message or attachments. Use this channel only for information needed for support; do not send passwords or personal photos. This information is used to answer your request and investigate reported problems, not for advertising or tracking profiles. Support messages are deleted within 90 days after the request is closed, unless retention is needed to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend rights. The email provider also processes information according to its own practices.

PUBLIC PAGES

When you open external support or privacy pages, your browser and hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address, needed to deliver the page. The project website does not install its own analytics or cookies. GitHub Pages hosting practices are described in GitHub's privacy policy. This policy is also available inside the app without opening the website.

YOUR CONTROLS AND REQUESTS

You can limit or revoke Photos permission in Settings, change the language, clear memory caches and logs, choose not to share information, and uninstall the app. For questions or requests about information sent to support, including access, correction or deletion where applicable, contact fhbcpbv@gmail.com. Support may request only the information needed to verify who is making the request. The responsible person has no remote access to your library or logs you have not sent.

CHANGES

Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and its update date. New features that change access, collection or sharing require a review of the privacy information before distribution.
