SpaceSweep Android privacy policy

Updated October 22, 2026.

Controller and contact

Felipe Henrique Baccaglini Custodio is responsible for SpaceSweep. For support or privacy requests, email fhbcpbv@gmail.com.

Photos, videos, and permissions

SpaceSweep requests access to photos and videos to display the library, metadata, thumbnails, and previews. On Android versions that offer selected-media access, the app respects that choice. You can change or revoke access in Android settings.

The app reads identifiers, file names, type, date, duration, resolution, relative path, and size reported by Android MediaStore to provide filters, sorting, previews, and selection. Media content and metadata are processed on the device. The app has no internet permission and does not automatically send your library or logs to the developer. It requires no account and includes no advertising or analytics.

File sizes and cloud services

Displayed size is the value MediaStore reports for each accessible file. It does not measure physical storage that will be recovered or add alternate and edited versions. Media held only by a cloud service may not appear. SpaceSweep does not request cloud credentials or manage cloud caches or remote copies.

Deletion

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review the selection in SpaceSweep and confirm permanent deletion in Android's system interface. For more than 2,000 items, Android presents separate batch confirmations. Cloud services may retain independent copies or trash folders.

Data stored on the device

The app stores your language preference and up to 400 diagnostic events from the last 7 days locally. Events include time, counts, and operation results; they do not include photos, file names, or media identifiers. Old events are removed when the log is read or updated. You can clear the log in the app and share it manually after reviewing the text. Sharing uses another app you choose.

Backup of SpaceSweep app data is disabled. Uninstalling removes local app data according to Android behavior without deleting photos or videos from the media library.

Support and public pages

If you email support, we receive your address and the information you include to answer and investigate your request. Do not send passwords or personal photos. Support messages are deleted within 90 days after the request is closed unless retention is needed for legal obligations or to establish, exercise, or defend rights. The email provider also processes information under its own practices.

When you open this page, your browser and GitHub Pages may process technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address. The site does not install its own analytics or cookies. The privacy text is also available inside the app.

Your controls

You can change media permission in Android settings, clear the log, stop sharing information, and uninstall the app. To request access, correction, or deletion of data sent to support, email fhbcpbv@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and in the update date.
